## How to Use This Book

*The Eight-Hour Entrepreneur* is the companion textbook for a four-week
virtual learning lab: four 2-hour live sessions separated by 7-day Field
Discovery Windows. You can read it alongside a live cohort or work through it
on your own. Each chapter builds on the ones before it, so reading in order
is recommended. The book includes:

- **17 Chapters** that walk through all 11 blocks of the CO.STARTERS Canvas —
  from customer discovery and problem definition, through alternatives,
  value propositions, and lean validation tests, to messaging, distribution,
  pricing, cost planning, a 30-day launch plan, and a 2-minute pitch
- **20 Interactive MicroSims** — browser-based tools such as a break-even
  calculator, a competitive landscape map, and a two-minute pitch timer that
  let you practice each method before you use it with real customers. Some
  are still being built; the colored dot beside each MicroSim in the
  navigation shows its current status.
- **Scout the Fox**, a learning guide who shows up at key moments with tips,
  warnings about common founder mistakes, and a push to go test your
  assumptions
- **A Learning Graph** of 300 concepts showing how every idea in the course
  depends on the ones before it
- **Search** available from any page using the search bar

The [Learning Graph](learning-graph/index.md) visualizes how concepts connect
across chapters. If you want to jump to a specific topic or check what you
need to know first, start there. The [Course Description](course-description.md)
lists the full learning outcomes.

## About the Author

![Valerie Lockhart](img/valerie-lockhart-headshot.jpg){ width="180px" align="right" }

Valerie Lockhart runs [MTN.org](https://mtn.org) and has spent the last 30
years providing executive management and operational functions for
nonprofits, small businesses, and corporations, including legal compliance
and human resources. MTN.org also hosts the online community hub that keeps
each cohort of this course connected between live sessions.

Valerie draws on more than 25 years of experience in photography,
videography, web development, search engine optimization (SEO), content
creation and management, graphic design, and writing and editing to produce
engaging training and educational resources for people who want to create
with technology. Her expertise spans information and communication
technology (ICT), media creation, and the intersection of art and technology.

She is passionate about access, individual rights, and freedom of
information and expression, and she works to foster a culture of inclusion,
collaboration, and innovation in the technology industry. That commitment is
why this book is open and free: every founder who arrives with an idea should
have the tools to test it, whatever their background or budget.

## How to Cite This Book

If you reference this textbook in academic work, program proposals, workshop
materials, or other publications, please use one of the following citation
formats.

**APA (7th edition)**

Lockhart, V. (2026). *The Eight-Hour Entrepreneur*. https://dmccreary.github.io/eight-hour-entrepreneur/

**Chicago (17th edition)**

Lockhart, Valerie. 2026. *The Eight-Hour Entrepreneur*. https://dmccreary.github.io/eight-hour-entrepreneur/.

**MLA (9th edition)**

Lockhart, Valerie. *The Eight-Hour Entrepreneur*. 2026, dmccreary.github.io/eight-hour-entrepreneur/.

**BibTeX**

```bibtex
@book{lockhart2026eighthour,
  title     = {The Eight-Hour Entrepreneur},
  author    = {Lockhart, Valerie},
  year      = {2026},
  url       = {https://dmccreary.github.io/eight-hour-entrepreneur/},
  note      = {Interactive intelligent textbook}
}
```

To cite a specific chapter, append the chapter number and title — for
example:

Lockhart, V. (2026). Chapter 1: Entrepreneurship, Lean Thinking & the Idea
Trap. In *The Eight-Hour Entrepreneur*.
https://dmccreary.github.io/eight-hour-entrepreneur/chapters/01-entrepreneurship-lean-thinking/
