# Character Sheet: Scout the Fox

The canonical identity document for Scout, the pedagogical
mascot for the **The Eight-Hour Entrepreneur** textbook. Every pose prompt and every
piece of AI-generated content involving this character must re-anchor to
the description below — it is the source of truth for visual and voice
consistency.

## Identity

- **Name:** Scout
- **Species:** Fox
- **Subject:** Entrepreneurship / Lean Startup Validation
- **Catchphrase:** "Let's find out for real!"

## Visual Description

- **Body color:** Warm fox orange — hex `#E8791A`
- **Accent color:** Indigo — hex `#3F51B5`
- **Clothing / accessories:** A small indigo field-notebook satchel slung across the chest (no hat); the satchel strap and flap are the only clothing item
- **Expression:** Bright, alert eyes; a confident half-smile; ears perked forward as if listening closely
- **Size proportion:** Small, icon-sized, compact and rounded proportions with a fluffy orange-and-cream tail
- **Art style:** Modern flat vector illustration, clean lines, flat colors, transparent background

## Personality

- Scrappy
- Resourceful
- Encouraging
- Curious

## Voice

- Plainspoken — avoids business jargon and buzzwords
- Redirects internal debate outward: asks "What did the customer actually say?" instead of speculating
- Uses light field/exploration metaphors ("Let's go knock on a door," "Time for a quick field test")
- Signature phrases: "Let's find out for real!", "Talk to a real person about that.", "Small test, real answer."

## Why This Mascot

A fox is clever, resourceful, and agile — exactly the qualities the course asks of a founder testing an idea against real-world friction rather than building in isolation, and fox-orange fur pairs naturally with the book's Material "orange" accent color. The name **Scout** ties the mascot directly to the course's signature pedagogical device, the "Field Discovery Window," so the character's very identity reinforces the course's most distinctive teaching method: get out and talk to real customers.
