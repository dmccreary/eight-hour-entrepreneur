## About This MicroSim

This course runs on a four-phase philosophy: **Clarity → Validation → Action → Launch**.
The MicroSim lets participants step through the phases at their own pace instead of
watching an animation they cannot pause. Each phase shows three things:

- **What it means:** a one-sentence definition of the phase.
- **Jordan's example:** what Jordan, the dog-grooming-van founder from Chapter 1,
  actually did in that phase.
- **Before you click Next:** a question that asks the learner to predict the next move
  before seeing it.

A curved return arrow from Validation back to Clarity, labeled *if evidence says pivot*,
shows that the phases form a cycle. Clicking **Next** on Launch loops back to Clarity for
the same reason: every launch produces new evidence that sharpens the next round.

## How to Use

1. The sim opens on **Clarity**. Read the definition and Jordan's example.
2. Answer the **Before you click Next** question in your head (or in the chat), then
   click **Next ▶**.
3. Click any phase box to jump straight to it. **◀ Previous** steps back.
4. Click the curved **if evidence says pivot** arrow to see why Validation can send you
   back to Clarity.
5. On Launch, the Next button becomes **Back to Clarity ↺** to show that the cycle repeats.

The left and right arrow keys also step through the phases once the sim has focus.
On narrow screens the four boxes stack vertically.

## Lesson Plan

### Audience
Adult early-stage entrepreneurs and aspiring founders in a live virtual cohort.

### Learning Objective
Participants will be able to explain, in their own words, the purpose of each of the four
phases (Clarity, Validation, Action, Launch) and how the phases connect into a repeating
cycle rather than a one-time sequence.

**Bloom Level:** Understand (L2). **Bloom Verb:** explain.

### Duration
10-15 minutes

### Prerequisites
- Chapter 1 reading on the Clarity → Validation → Action → Launch philosophy
- Familiarity with Jordan's mobile dog-grooming example

### Activities

1. **Guided walk-through (5 min):** The facilitator shares the sim and pauses on each
   phase. Before each click of Next, participants type their prediction into the chat.
2. **Pivot scenario (3 min):** Click the return arrow. Ask: what evidence would send
   Jordan back to Clarity? What would change in Jordan's one-sentence guess?
3. **Apply to your own idea (5 min):** In breakout rooms, each participant writes their
   own one-line example for Clarity and Validation, using Jordan's examples as a model.

### Assessment
- Can the participant explain each phase without reading the definition off the screen?
- Can the participant describe a piece of evidence that would trigger a pivot back to Clarity?
- Does the participant describe the phases as a cycle rather than a checklist?
