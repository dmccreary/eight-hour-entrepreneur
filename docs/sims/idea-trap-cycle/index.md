## About This MicroSim

The idea trap is hard to spot from the inside because it looks like diligence. This
MicroSim puts the two ways a founder can spend the next hour side by side. On the left,
**Unrefined Business Idea** and **Refine the Plan** form a closed loop: pitch decks,
logos, and spreadsheets feel like progress, but the arrow always leads back to the same
unanswered question. On the right, a straight path runs from the idea to a
**Real-World Test** and ends at **Validated Learning**, a real answer of yes or no.

The example follows Jordan, the founder from Chapter 1 who wants to drive a
dog-grooming van to customers' homes. Two counters make the contrast concrete:
**Hours invested** climbs on both paths, but **Real customer answers** only moves when
Jordan runs a real-world test.

## How to Use

1. **Click any box** to read what it means, with Jordan's version of it.
2. **Hover over (or tap) an arrow.** The loop and the forward path each explain
   what kind of path they are.
3. **Click Refine the Plan** several times. Watch Jordan circle the loop while hours
   pile up and customer answers stay at zero.
4. **Click Test with Customers.** Jordan moves down the forward path, and each test
   adds real answers, including answers of "no."
5. **Click Reset** to start over.

On narrow screens the loop sits on top and the forward path runs down the right side.

## Lesson Plan

### Audience
Adult early-stage entrepreneurs and aspiring founders in a live virtual cohort.

### Learning Objective
Participants will be able to differentiate productive early-stage activity (real-world
testing) from unproductive activity (endless refinement) and recognize when they are
personally in the idea trap.

**Bloom Level:** Analyze (L4). **Bloom Verb:** differentiate.

### Duration
10 minutes

### Prerequisites
- Chapter 1 reading on unrefined concepts and the idea trap
- An unrefined business idea of their own

### Activities

1. **Explore (3 min):** Individually, click all four boxes and hover both arrows.
2. **Predict and test (3 min):** Before touching the buttons, predict how many times
   Jordan must click Refine the Plan before the customer-answers counter moves. Then try
   it, and compare with one click of Test with Customers.
3. **Breakout discussion (4 min):** In breakout rooms, each person lists the last three
   things they did for their own idea and sorts each one onto the loop or the forward path.

### Assessment
- Can the participant explain why refining a plan never changes the customer-answers count?
- Can the participant name one "forward path" action they will take before the next session?
- Does the participant recognize that a "no" from a real customer is still validated learning?
