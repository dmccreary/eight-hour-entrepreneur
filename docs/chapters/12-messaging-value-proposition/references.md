# References: Messaging & the Value Proposition Statement

1. [Value proposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Value_proposition) - Wikipedia - Defines a value proposition as the combination of benefit and price a company promises customers, and how firms use it to differentiate themselves. Grounds this chapter's one-sentence value proposition in the underlying concept.

2. [Elevator pitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elevator_pitch) - Wikipedia - Traces the elevator pitch's origins and covers frameworks for delivering a short, clear spoken pitch in under two minutes. Directly supports this chapter's elevator pitch concept.

3. [Copywriting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copywriting) - Wikipedia - Comprehensive overview of persuasive writing for marketing, including history, notable practitioners, and formats across traditional and digital media. Relevant background for sales copy, headline writing, and persuasive writing.

4. Building a StoryBrand (2017) - Donald Miller - HarperCollins Leadership - Miller pioneered the "if you confuse, you lose" principle and a story-based framework that positions the customer as the hero, which directly clarifies this chapter's message-clarity and story-based-messaging concepts.

5. Obviously Awesome (2019) - April Dunford - Ambient Press - Dunford developed a repeatable, five-component positioning process that produces a plain-language statement of what a company does and for whom, the same discipline underlying this chapter's one-sentence value proposition template.

6. [How to Create an Effective Value Proposition](https://online.hbs.edu/blog/post/creating-a-value-proposition) - Harvard Business School Online - Walks through identifying the customer's core problem and expressing your differentiation in one concise statement, supporting this chapter's one-sentence value proposition process.

7. [Ad-Lib Value Proposition Template](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/ad-lib-value-proposition-template) - Strategyzer - A fill-in-the-blank exercise for rapidly drafting several value proposition sentences, functionally similar to this chapter's own value-proposition sentence template.

8. [Testing Visual Design: A Comprehensive Guide](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/testing-visual-design/) - Nielsen Norman Group - Explains the five-second test as a first-impression method for checking whether a design or message is understood immediately, the same technique this chapter recommends for outsider comprehension testing.

9. [Win the Business with This Elevator Pitch](https://hbr.org/2012/08/win-the-business-with-this-ele) - Harvard Business Review - A worked example of building a compelling, concise pitch in under a minute for someone who has never heard of the business, illustrating the elevator pitch in practice.

10. [15 Tips for Writing Killer Sales Copy](https://www.entrepreneur.com/growing-a-business/15-tips-for-writing-killer-sales-copy/306961) - Entrepreneur.com - Practical tips for persuasive sales copy, including clear calls to action and cutting unnecessary words, reinforcing this chapter's sales copy, call-to-action, and message-simplicity concepts.
