# References: Revenue Models & Pricing

1. [Revenue model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revenue_model) - Wikipedia - Explains how businesses structure income generation, covering revenue stream types, pricing mechanisms, and the difference between one-time and recurring income patterns central to this chapter's revenue model discussion.

2. [Pricing strategy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pricing_strategy) - Wikipedia - Surveys dozens of pricing approaches including cost-plus, value-based, penetration, and tiered pricing, plus the psychology of price sensitivity, directly supporting this chapter's comparison of pricing models.

3. [Break-even point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Break-even_point) - Wikipedia - Details the formula and assumptions behind calculating the sales volume needed to cover fixed and variable costs, the exact discipline behind this chapter's break-even thinking and calculator.

4. Business Model Generation - Alexander Osterwalder & Yves Pigneur - Wiley - Originated the "Revenue Streams" canvas block and its taxonomy of pricing mechanisms (fixed menu, tiered, subscription, usage-based), giving founders a structured way to enumerate the pricing model options this chapter compares.

5. The Lean Startup - Eric Ries - Crown Business - Popularized rigorous split-testing of pricing assumptions through real customer behavior rather than surveys, the direct ancestor of this chapter's price point testing and revenue-assumption discipline.

6. [Pricing Strategy - HBR Topic Hub](https://hbr.org/topic/subject/pricing-strategy) - Harvard Business Review - A curated collection of HBR articles, case studies, and tools on pricing decisions, offering real company examples of value-based and dynamic pricing that extend this chapter's pricing-model comparison.

7. [Is It Time to Rethink Your Pricing Strategy?](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/is-it-time-to-rethink-your-pricing-strategy/) - MIT Sloan Management Review - Argues that pricing power is a learned organizational skill, contrasting cost-based, competition-based, and value-based pricing approaches that map directly onto this chapter's two ways to set a price.

8. [The Business Model Canvas](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-business-model-canvas) - Strategyzer - Describes the nine-block Business Model Canvas, showing how revenue streams and cost structure depend on the customer and value blocks above them, reinforcing this chapter's revenue-model framing.

9. [Startup pricing 101](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/6h-startup-pricing-101) - Y Combinator Startup Library - A founder-facing primer on setting an initial price, covering common underpricing mistakes and how pricing choices shape customer acquisition, complementing this chapter's guidance on choosing a pricing model.

10. [An Entrepreneur's Guide to Startup Pricing Strategies](https://www.entrepreneur.com/money-finance/an-entrepreneurs-guide-to-startup-pricing-strategies/428342) - Entrepreneur.com - Covers market-based pricing strategies and recommends testing prices against customer willingness to pay, closely mirroring this chapter's price point testing and pricing validation process.
