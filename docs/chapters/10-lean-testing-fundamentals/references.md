# References: Lean Testing Fundamentals

1. [Lean startup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lean_startup) - Wikipedia - Overview of the lean startup methodology, including the build-measure-learn loop, minimum viable products, and validated learning. Provides the broader framework behind this chapter's lean validation practice.

2. [Minimum viable product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimum_viable_product) - Wikipedia - Explains the MVP concept coined by Frank Robinson and popularized by Steve Blank and Eric Ries, including testing approaches and criticisms. Grounds the chapter's narrower "minimum testable iteration" idea.

3. [Customer development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Customer_development) - Wikipedia - Describes Steve Blank's four-step methodology for testing business model hypotheses with real customers before building. Underpins the chapter's emphasis on real-world evidence over assumptions.

4. Testing Business Ideas (2019) - David J. Bland & Alexander Osterwalder - Wiley - Bland and Osterwalder catalog 44 named experiment types, including the concierge test and the pre-order test, giving precise, reusable names to exactly the manual-service and pre-sale formats this chapter teaches.

5. Running Lean (3rd Edition) - Ash Maurya - O'Reilly Media - Maurya pioneered the "ask for the sale" and traction-testing sequence within the Lean Canvas methodology, showing founders how to prove demand manually before writing a line of code, mirroring the chapter's manual-before-automated rule.

6. [Why the Lean Start-Up Changes Everything](https://hbr.org/2013/05/why-the-lean-start-up-changes-everything) - Harvard Business Review - Steve Blank's HBR article explaining why lean, hypothesis-driven experimentation replaces the traditional business plan for reducing the risk of building something no one will pay for.

7. [The Experiment Library](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/experiment-library) - Strategyzer - A library of dozens of business experiments organized by cost, time, and strength of evidence, giving founders a menu of test formats beyond the three covered directly in this chapter.

8. [An MVP Is Not a Cheaper Product, It's About Smart Learning](https://steveblank.com/2013/07/22/an-mvp-is-not-a-cheaper-product-its-about-smart-learning/) - Steve Blank - Argues that a minimum viable product exists to test customer demand cheaply, not to save engineering effort, reinforcing why a minimum testable iteration is scoped around a single question.

9. [The Startup Roadmap](https://www.score.org/startup-roadmap) - SCORE - A free, structured course walking founders through validating a business idea before committing money, paralleling this chapter's sequence from test design to kill decision.

10. [Do Things That Don't Scale](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/96-do-things-that-don-t-scale) - Y Combinator Startup Library - Paul Graham's essay urging founders to do manual, unscalable work early to learn faster, directly illustrating the chapter's manual-before-automated principle.
