# Chapters

This textbook is organized into 17 chapters covering 300 concepts.

## Chapter Overview

1. [Entrepreneurship, Lean Thinking & the Idea Trap](01-entrepreneurship-lean-thinking/index.md) - Introduces what an early-stage venture is, why unrefined ideas fall into the "idea trap," and the Clarity → Validation → Action → Launch philosophy that grounds every decision made in the course.
2. [Founder Mindset & the Cohort Learning Experience](02-founder-mindset-cohort/index.md) - Covers the psychological-safety norms, peer cohort structure, and Field Discovery Window rhythm that make the course's virtual learning lab format work, plus the founder mindsets it aims to build.
3. [The CO.STARTERS Canvas Framework](03-costarters-canvas-framework/index.md) - Introduces the CO.STARTERS Canvas as the course's organizing framework and walks through each of its 11 building blocks that later chapters explore in depth.
4. [Customer Discovery & Segmentation](04-customer-discovery-segmentation/index.md) - Teaches how to identify and define a target customer, from broad market segments down to a specific ideal-customer profile, persona, and avatar that focuses the rest of the venture design.
5. [Defining the Real Problem](05-defining-the-real-problem/index.md) - Guides founders from a vague pain point to a validated, prioritized problem statement, distinguishing real problems from perceived ones and root causes from symptoms.
6. [Designing the Solution & Initial Offer](06-designing-solution-offer/index.md) - Shows how to turn a validated problem into a minimum viable offer, iterating on solution scope and clarity before any customer interviews take place.
7. [Alternatives & the Competitive Landscape](07-alternatives-competitive-landscape/index.md) - Maps how customers currently solve the problem today, from direct competitors to do-nothing alternatives, to reveal genuine gaps in the market.
8. [Value Proposition: Benefit & Advantage](08-value-proposition-benefit-advantage/index.md) - Builds the venture's value proposition by articulating why the customer wants the solution (benefit) and why this founder is best positioned to deliver it (advantage).
9. [Discovery Interviews & the Reality Check](09-discovery-interviews-reality-check/index.md) - Teaches how to design and run unbiased customer discovery interviews, culminating in the Reality Check pressure test that surfaces real pain versus assumed pain.
10. [Lean Testing Fundamentals](10-lean-testing-fundamentals/index.md) - Introduces the core lean-validation moves — service before product, manual before automated, pre-selling before building — and how to design a minimum testable iteration.
11. [Interpreting Validation Evidence](11-interpreting-validation-evidence/index.md) - Covers how to read lean-test results and decide whether to kill, pivot, or persevere with a concept, based on real evidence of demand and customer payment signals.
12. [Messaging & the Value Proposition Statement](12-messaging-value-proposition/index.md) - Turns a validated concept into a one-sentence value proposition and high-conversion sales copy that a stranger can understand in five seconds.
13. [Distribution & Go-to-Market Channels](13-distribution-go-to-market/index.md) - Explores how a venture's product or service actually reaches its customer, from word of mouth to online, partnership, and marketplace channels.
14. [Revenue Models & Pricing](14-revenue-models-pricing/index.md) - Covers how ventures make money, from choosing and validating a pricing model to testing willingness to pay and projecting early revenue.
15. [Startup Needs & Cost Planning](15-startup-needs-cost-planning/index.md) - Separates one-time startup needs from ongoing costs and builds basic financial-planning literacy — budget, cash flow awareness, and cost tracking — for a lean venture.
16. [Building the 30-Day Launch Plan](16-thirty-day-launch-plan/index.md) - Synthesizes the completed canvas into a concrete 30-day launch roadmap defining what to sell, who to sell to, how to reach them, and weekly milestones.
17. [Pitching, Accountability & What Comes Next](17-pitching-accountability-next-steps/index.md) - Closes the course with the two-minute peer pitch, public commitment to 30-day goals, and the accountability network and community resources that sustain momentum after graduation.

## How to Use This Textbook

Chapters are ordered so that every concept appears after all of its prerequisites -- read them in sequence on a first pass. The learning graph in the [Learning Graph](../learning-graph/index.md) section shows the full dependency structure if you want to jump ahead to a specific topic.

**Note:** Each chapter includes a list of concepts covered. Make sure to complete prerequisites before moving to advanced chapters.
