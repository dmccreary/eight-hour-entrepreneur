# References: The CO.STARTERS Canvas Framework

1. [Business Model Canvas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_Model_Canvas) - Wikipedia - Explains the nine-block strategic template developed by Osterwalder and Pigneur, the exact tool this chapter contrasts with the CO.STARTERS Canvas to show how the two frameworks serve different stages of a venture.

2. [Business plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_plan) - Wikipedia - Covers the structure and purpose of a traditional multi-page business plan, giving readers the point of comparison this chapter uses to explain why the CO.STARTERS Canvas deliberately fits on a single page.

3. [Value proposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Value_proposition) - Wikipedia - Traces the value-proposition concept and its planning frameworks, relevant background for the canvas's Benefit and Advantage blocks that this chapter introduces as the building blocks of a venture's value proposition.

4. Business Model Generation - Alexander Osterwalder and Yves Pigneur - Wiley - Osterwalder and Pigneur pioneered the nine-block Business Model Canvas itself, the direct ancestor this chapter uses as its comparison point for explaining what the CO.STARTERS Canvas simplifies and why.

5. Running Lean (3rd Edition) - Ash Maurya - O'Reilly Media - Maurya pioneered the Lean Canvas, a one-page adaptation built for unproven early-stage ventures rather than operating businesses, the same design philosophy this chapter credits for the CO.STARTERS Canvas's blank-page starting point.

6. [The Business Model Canvas](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-business-model-canvas) - Strategyzer - The creators' own resource page for the Business Model Canvas, offering a free downloadable template and explanation of its nine blocks, useful for comparing directly against the CO.STARTERS Canvas's eleven blocks.

7. [Business Model Canvas](https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/management/business-model-canvas/) - Corporate Finance Institute - A practical walkthrough of the Business Model Canvas's nine components with worked examples from Toyota and Amazon, reinforcing this chapter's explanation of how a canvas format visualizes an entire business on one page.

8. [How to Do a Practical Lean Business Plan in Four Easy Steps](https://www.sba.gov/blog/how-do-practical-lean-business-plan-four-easy-steps) - U.S. Small Business Administration - Describes a one-page, four-step lean planning format as an alternative to a lengthy formal plan, directly supporting this chapter's point that a single page can capture a venture's whole logic.

9. [8 Types of Business Models & the Value They Deliver](https://online.hbs.edu/blog/post/types-of-business-models) - Harvard Business School Online - Surveys distinct business models and how each creates value, useful background for filling in a canvas's Revenue and Costs blocks once a venture's basic model is chosen.

10. [Lean Canvas](https://leanspark.ai/leancanvas) - LEANSpark - Built by Ash Maurya, this tool explains exactly which four Business Model Canvas blocks the Lean Canvas replaces with startup-specific ones, concretely illustrating this chapter's comparison between the two canvas families.
