# References: Value Proposition: Benefit & Advantage

1. [Value Proposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Value_proposition) - Wikipedia - Explains how a business states the value it promises to deliver to customers, the umbrella concept this chapter builds from benefit and advantage into a single value statement.
2. [Competitive Advantage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Competitive_advantage) - Wikipedia - Covers the attributes that let a firm outperform its rivals, the concept this chapter narrows into founder advantage and the stronger unfair advantage.
3. [Unique Selling Proposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unique_selling_proposition) - Wikipedia - Describes the practice of naming the one factor that makes an offer different from and better than competitors, directly matching this chapter's unique selling point.
4. Value Proposition Design - Alexander Osterwalder, Yves Pigneur, Greg Bernarda, Alan Smith - Wiley - Credited for the Value Proposition Canvas's "gain creators" and "pain relievers" framework, which sharpens this chapter's distinction between a functional benefit and an emotional benefit.
5. Zero to One - Peter Thiel - Crown Business - Credited for popularizing proprietary technology, network effects, and branding as durable, hard-to-copy sources of edge, clarifying this chapter's line between an ordinary competitive advantage and a true unfair advantage.
6. [The Value Proposition Canvas](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-value-proposition-canvas) - Strategyzer - Introduces the tool for mapping customer jobs, pains, and gains against an offer's features, a practical method for the benefit articulation this chapter teaches.
7. [The Elements of Value](https://hbr.org/2016/09/the-elements-of-value) - Harvard Business Review - Identifies universal functional, emotional, and social building blocks of customer value, giving concrete categories that extend this chapter's functional-benefit-versus-emotional-benefit distinction.
8. [What Is Competitive Advantage?](https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/management/competitive-advantage/) - Corporate Finance Institute - Defines competitive advantage as an edge that cannot be easily replicated by rivals, the same durability test this chapter applies when separating an unfair advantage from an ordinary one.
9. [Unique Selling Proposition (USP)](https://www.entrepreneur.com/encyclopedia/unique-selling-proposition-usp) - Entrepreneur Small Business Encyclopedia - Explains how a seller states the one reason its offer is different and better than the competition, a plain-language model for this chapter's unique selling point.
10. [How to Create an Effective Value Proposition](https://online.hbs.edu/blog/post/creating-a-value-proposition) - Harvard Business School Online - Walks through combining a customer's job to be done with a founder's differentiation into one clear statement, closely matching this chapter's value statement pattern.
