    Cover Image Prompt¶

    Please generate a professional-quality cover image for this textbook. This image will be used in social media previews and must follow the formatting guidelines for an Open Graph image preview.

    Required specifications: - Format: PNG - Wide-landscape format - Size: 1200x630 pixels (1.91:1 aspect ratio) - This is the Open Graph standard for social media previews

    The image has four layers, back to front: background montage, color treatment, mascot, and title text.

    Subject & Tone¶

    The Eight-Hour Entrepreneur is a textbook about an intensive, virtual "learning lab" that takes early-stage entrepreneurs from an unrefined idea to a validated, launch-ready venture in 8 total hours, using the CO.STARTERS Canvas framework, real customer discovery interviews, and lean validation testing. The intended audience is early-stage entrepreneurs and aspiring founders in a cohort-based, adult-professional-development setting. The visual tone should be energetic and scrappy-but-professional — approachable and action-oriented rather than corporate-stiff, reflecting the course's hands-on "get out and test it" philosophy.

    Title¶

    Place The Eight-Hour Entrepreneur in the center of the image, in a clean, highly legible sans-serif font. Use a light/white font color with a subtle drop shadow or dark scrim behind it so it stays readable against the busy montage background. Keep the title short enough to render at a large size — do not shrink it to fit if the title is long; instead simplify the background directly behind the text.

    Background Montage¶

    Arrange a montage of the following 8 concepts around the title, each rendered in a consistent illustration style (see Style below) so the composition reads as one image rather than a collage of unrelated styles:

    • CO.STARTERS Canvas — a colorful worksheet grid covered in sticky-note-like blocks (Customer, Problem, Solution, Revenue, etc.), warm and hand-filled-looking
    • Discovery Interview — two video-call windows in conversation; one figure leaning in and taking notes on a small notepad
    • Reality Check Pressure Test — a hand gently pressing a magnifying glass over a sticky note with a question mark on it, symbolizing testing an assumption against reality
    • Pre-Sale Offer (Lean Test) — a simple handwritten "Coming Soon" sign taped to a door, next to a small order clipboard with one checkmark
    • One-Sentence Value Proposition — a glowing speech bubble containing a short, bold headline (a few abstract short line-strokes standing in for text, not literal readable paragraphs)
    • Distribution Channel — a central product icon with three arrows radiating outward to a small storefront icon, an envelope icon, and a phone icon
    • Revenue Model — a simple upward-trending bar chart topped with a dollar sign
    • Thirty-Day Launch Plan — a 30-day calendar grid with a small rocket icon marking the final day

    Mascot¶

    Place the book's mascot, Scout the Fox, in the lower-left corner, sized so it does not overlap the title text. Scout is a warm fox-orange fox with a cream underbelly and a fluffy orange-and-cream tail, wearing a small indigo field-notebook satchel slung across the chest (no hat), with bright alert eyes and a confident half-smile, ears perked forward. Show Scout in a welcoming pose — waving cheerfully with one paw, as if inviting the reader in. Rendered in the same flat-vector style as the montage elements, not photorealistic.

    Style & Composition¶

    • Illustration style: modern flat vector — clean lines, flat colors, no photorealism. Apply this consistently to every montage element and to the mascot.
    • Color palette: deep indigo and warm fox-orange, with cream/off-white highlights — this matches both the book's site theme (indigo primary, orange accent) and the mascot's own coloring.
    • Lighting/mood: bright and energetic, optimistic.
    • Composition: title centered, montage elements arranged in a loose ring or grid around it, mascot in the lower-left, generous negative space immediately behind the title so it stays readable.

    Avoid¶

    • Do not render dense paragraphs of illegible text anywhere in the image.
    • Avoid generic stock-photo cliches (handshakes, isolated lightbulbs, people pointing at whiteboards) — none of these are in the chosen montage list above.
    • Avoid photorealistic human faces — keep any human figures abstract/stylized, consistent with the flat-vector style.
    • Do not let montage elements visually compete with or overlap the title.
