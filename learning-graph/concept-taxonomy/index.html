    Concept Taxonomy¶

    The 300 concepts in the learning graph are organized into 14 categories, each evenly distributed (no category exceeds 10% of the total) and mapped to the course's week-by-week structure and the CO.STARTERS Canvas framework.

    Categories¶

    FOUND — Entrepreneurship Foundations & Philosophy¶

    The mindset, vocabulary, and course philosophy underlying the whole program: what an early-stage venture is, the idea trap, the Clarity → Validation → Action → Launch philosophy, and the psychological-safety norms of the cohort.

    CANV — CO.STARTERS Canvas Framework¶

    The Business Model Canvas adaptation used throughout the course and its 11 individual blocks (Customer, Problem, Solution, Alternatives, Benefit, Advantage, Message, Distribution, Revenue, Startup Needs, Costs).

    CUST — Customer Discovery & Segmentation¶

    Identifying and defining who the venture serves: target customer, personas, market segments, and customer prioritization.

    PROB — Problem Definition & Pain Points¶

    Defining the real problem a customer has: pain points, root causes, problem severity/frequency, and problem validation.

    SOL — Solution Design & Offer Development¶

    Framing what is offered to solve the validated problem: initial offer, minimum viable offer, and solution iteration.

    ALT — Alternatives & Competitive Analysis¶

    How customers currently solve the problem: competitors, workarounds, substitutes, and competitive positioning.

    VALU — Value Proposition: Benefit & Advantage¶

    Why the customer wants the solution and why this founder is positioned to deliver it: benefit, advantage, and value-proposition articulation.

    INTV — Discovery Interviews & Research Methods¶

    Techniques for talking to real customers: interview design, the Reality Check pressure test, and synthesizing findings.

    VALID — Lean Validation & Testing¶

    Testing assumptions cheaply before building: lean tests, pre-selling, manual service delivery, and kill/pivot/persevere decisions.

    MSG — Messaging & Communication¶

    Turning a validated concept into clear, compelling communication: value-proposition statements, sales copy, and founder narrative.

    DIST — Distribution & Go-to-Market¶

    How the product or service reaches the customer: channel selection, word of mouth, and multi-channel strategy.

    REV — Revenue Models & Pricing¶

    How the venture makes money: pricing models, pricing validation, and willingness to pay.

    FIN — Startup Needs & Cost Planning¶

    One-time startup needs versus ongoing costs, and basic financial-planning literacy for a lean venture.

    LAUNCH — Launch Planning, Pitching & Accountability¶

    Synthesizing the canvas into a 30-day launch roadmap, pitching to peers, and the accountability structures that sustain momentum after the course.
